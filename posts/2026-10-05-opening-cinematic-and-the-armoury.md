Three things landed since the last entry: the opening cinematic, two more finished heads for the named cast, and a look at the full arms and armour set, most of which has not been shown on this log before.

## The opening cinematic

The game begins inside Brother Cadoc's Chronicle. A candle is lit, a quill is laid down, and the manuscript page comes alive in iron-gall ink and red lead: the sun with a grey disc over it, the black-eared wheat, a starving child, the two armoured figures at Camlann, and the dead pushing up through the turf.

The first cut runs 45 seconds and is also on the [home page](index.html#opening). It sets the visual language for every history sequence in the game: drawn, flat, Insular, and animated in two planes.

## Iddon, complete

Iddon the smith is the first head built for the new large male body. Since the last entry he got his skin, a single scalp scar running from the crown to his left temple, a grey-brown fringe and full beard as swappable hair cards, a face rig, and 52 ARKit facial shapes. The beard follows the jaw when he talks.

## Brother Tysilio, complete

![Brother Tysilio, built head with hair and beard, front and side](assets/img/gallery/tysilio_hair_beard.jpg)

Tysilio is 74, a hermit, and the last of a burned monastic house of readers. The head brief called for deep lines, sun-dark skin, one milky eye and very few teeth, and all of that is in the build:

- **Skin.** Age spots at three scales, deep forehead furrows, crepe skin, watery red lid rims, grey-white brows and stubble.
- **Eyes.** One brown amber eye and one clouded with a cataract, with only a ghost of a pupil.
- **Teeth.** Ten of twenty-eight teeth kept, yellowed, set behind an old overbite.
- **Hair.** Long wavy white hair over a fuzz-covered old tonsure and a long V beard, cut down to about 16,000 quads after the first pass came in at double that.
- **Face rig.** 52 ARKit facial shapes, with the beard following the jaw.

![Brother Tysilio, skin close-up with the cataract eye](assets/img/gallery/tysilio_skin_close.jpg)

## Cynan, in progress

Cynan's head fit is approved and his expression topology is built. The AI source had very small features for its skull, so the fit was scaled and pulled back until his ears sat where the base body expects them, which fixed a forward-leaning neck. His upper right incisor is chipped, cut into the tooth mesh itself, so the gap shows when he talks.

![Cynan, expression topology, eye, mouth and teeth](assets/img/gallery/cynan_topology.jpg)

## The armoury

The weapons track closed on September 30 with 31 Unreal-ready packages. Most of it has not been on this log yet. Every item has collision, attachment sockets, a mass value and 2K textures, and the renders below come straight from the masters.

![Spathas for Bedwyr, Owain and Gwalchmai](assets/img/gallery/arms_spathas.jpg)

- **Swords.** A basic spatha and four named ones: Bedwyr's, Owain's, Gwalchmai's and a gold-hilted ring-sword.
- **Seaxes.** Basic, bone-handled and long, each with a riveted leather sheath.
- **Spears.** War spear, hunting spear, throwing spear and an angon, plus four loose spear heads.
- **Missile weapons.** A 1.75 m yew bow and a sling, both rigged, with broadhead and bodkin arrows, a leather quiver and sling stones.
- **Shield.** One round limewood shield with seven painted designs and two damage states.
- **Kit.** A boar-crest spangenhelm, a war horn worn on the hip or raised to blow, and gold and silver arm-rings.
