HERO1 is the tall base body for the player character. At 1.93 m he stands a head above most of the manor, which the folk will comment on.

## Highlights

- **Hands.** A scanned reference hand replaced the first procedural hands, then the forearms were rebuilt as a smooth straight taper and mirrored so both arms match.
- **Face.** Reshaped lips, a rebuilt mouth interior with procedural teeth, and new eyelid topology so blinks no longer tear.
- **Expressions.** Shape keys for blinks, smiles, frowns, pucker, brow moves and jaw open, tested in combinations from a wink to a yell.
- **Budget.** The body was cut to about 58,000 triangles while protecting the head, hands, knees and feet.
- **Skeleton.** Auto-fitted from landmarks, hand-aligned on one side, mirrored, weighted and baked from the A-pose to the T-pose Unreal expects.

## Four hairstyles

1. Swept-back undercut
2. Medium wavy, middle part
3. Half-up high ponytail
4. Shoulder-length loose waves

The character bible picks the wavy style as Owain's default and the longer style from Part Two onward, so time visibly passes on him.
