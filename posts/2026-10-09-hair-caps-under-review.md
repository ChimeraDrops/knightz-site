title: Dev Log #16: Hair caps under review
date: 2026-10-09
---

Every hero now gets a hand-checked hair cap before anything goes into Unreal. Five of the fifteen are approved.

## Rules set today

- **Scalp edges.** Cap edges rest on the scalp and never float above the skin.
- **Neck closure.** The back of the cap closes cleanly at the nape with no gap to the neck.
- **Silhouette.** The outline of the hair is checked from the front, side and back against the reference.
- **Budget.** About 1,200 triangles per cap.

## Tools

Blender audit and rebuild tools now check every cap against the rules and rebuild any that fail, so a fix on one character carries to the rest.

## Process

Review runs one hero at a time. No FBX export or Unreal work happens until all fifteen are approved.

## Next

Review the remaining ten heroes: six on the standard body and four on the large body.
