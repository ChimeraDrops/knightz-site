Nine more of Owain's clips are approved: the last three parries, all five broken parries and the first blocks. The audit stands at 70 of Owain's 76 original clips. Six remain.

![Every parry and broken parry, beat by beat](assets/img/gallery/devlog53_parry_beats_1116.png)

## The rest of the parries

A parry is a block pressed just before a hit lands. The shield knocks the attack aside and wears down the enemy's guard. Owain is back in guard and can act at once.

- **Double swipe.** Two contacts, on frames 5 and 12. The first is on his left and the second on his right, in the same order the enemy swings. Back in guard on frame 26.
- **Heavy.** This one answers the two-handed overhead blow from the undead. The shield rises over his head and to the left, angled up. He steps 25 cm to his right as the blow lands on frame 9, so it glances off the shield and away from his head.
- **Grab or bite.** Against a squat and lunge, the shield drops low and drives forward into the grabber's face and chest on frame 6, then sweeps the grabber aside to his left. He steps 12 cm forward.
- **Checks.** All three pass the joint check and the mesh scan.

## Broken parries

When the enemy's guard meter is already broken, the same parry press does more. Owain deflects the attack, then bashes the enemy with the shield. The enemy is left stunned in place, arms slumped and rocking for a few seconds. That opens the window for a severing blow.

- **Two beats.** Every broken parry starts with the matching parry, then pulls the shield back to the chest and drives it straight forward. The bash lands about 90 cm in front of where he started, at chest height, with the face of the shield square to the enemy.
- **Footwork.** The left foot steps in with the bash and the right foot follows. Most of the set ends 15 cm forward. The heavy ends 25 cm right and 15 cm forward. The grab or bite takes two short advances for 24 cm.
- **Five clips.** Single swipe from the right, single swipe from the left, double swipe, heavy and grab or bite. They run from 28 to 39 frames.
- **Grab or bite.** Here the shove stops the lunge and holds for two frames with no sweep aside, so the enemy stays in front for the bash.
- **Checks.** All five pass the joint check and the mesh scan.

## Blocks

![The first three blocks and the audit count](assets/img/gallery/devlog53_blocks_audit_1116.png)

A block is the shield held up early, outside the parry window. It costs stamina and stops the damage, but does nothing to the enemy's guard. The enemy recoils.

- **Swipe from his right or left.** Two clips of 18 frames, in place. The shield shifts to meet the blow on frame 4 and is driven back about 20 cm. His head rocks back. He is back in guard on frame 14.
- **Double swipe.** Two blows, on frames 4 and 11, first on his left, then on his right. Each one drives the shield and his head back. He gives up 15 cm of ground in two steps and is back in guard on frame 24.

## Changes to the plan

- **No shield pin.** The earlier plan had Owain pin the enemy with his shield after a broken parry. That is out. He parries, bashes and stuns instead.
- **Broken parry is two beats:** the deflect first, then a separate shield bash.
- **Blocks split by side** like the parries: five clips in the set, single swipe from the right, single swipe from the left, double swipe, heavy and grab or bite.
- **Enemy clips to match:** a stunned clip for each broken parry and a recoil for each block.
