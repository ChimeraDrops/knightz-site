A big day for the cast.

## Complete

- **Sir Bedwyr.** Final touch-ups to brows, hooded lids, lip colour and deeper wrinkles, then 52 ARKit facial shapes for full facial animation.

## Owain

The player character's head is on the healthy male base body with skin textured and a slicked-back hair asset approved. Sideburns live in the hair asset, so every hairstyle can change them.

## A bigger body

Iddon the smith is barrel-chested, so he gets a new 1.85 m large male body. It reuses the healthy body's topology, hands, feet and neck seam, which keeps every head tool working on it. The body is rigged, weighted, baked to the bind pose and textured. Iddon's head has its fit approved and is now in the expression stage.
