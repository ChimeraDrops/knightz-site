A day away from Blender to write down the whole game.

## Four documents

- **Screenplay.** Prologue, twelve chapters across three years, an epilogue, 45 quests, every choice tagged with the flags it sets, and the ending matrix.
- **Character bible.** Twenty-plus named characters with age, temperament, period costume, build specifications and production priority.
- **Economy and management.** The time model, people, jobs, stores and buildings, the steward's planner and the Winter Night maths.
- **World, terrain and props.** Eleven region maps, how they connect, sky and season states, and 149 prop lines with priorities.

## Terrain

Each of the eleven regions has a heightmap at 2017 x 2017 pixels in 16-bit, thirteen masks (forest, field, meadow, moor, marsh, road, ruin and more), an annotated layout map and the exact scale values for import into Unreal. They are authored from real Shropshire geography and will be upgraded to survey-grade elevation data before final art.

## Decisions made

Long winters, one clock everywhere, messengers carry orders from the field, and from spring to autumn the main human threat is a brigand band holding an old hillfort in the Clee Hills.
