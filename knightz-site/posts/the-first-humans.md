---
title: Dev Log #3: The first humans of a hungry manor
date: 2026-09-27
---

The manor's people are hungry. The first human bodies reflect that: an emaciated male peasant (MEP1) and female peasant (FEP1), plus undead twins of both.

## Bodies

The female body was made by conforming the male body, scaled to 1.70 m, onto the female sculpt. The fit held at under a millimetre for most of the surface, with the hands carried over as rigid parts so the fingers stay clean.

## Clothing

- Male: tunic, belt, trousers and foot wraps.
- Female: shift, gown, apron, belt, coif and foot wraps, built as real layers so each can be removed without leaving baked shadows behind.
- Every garment is its own asset and swaps one to one between the living and undead versions.

## Hair

Four card-based hairstyles per body, each a separate asset with a tint parameter. One style set can dress a whole village in different colours.

## Dirt, wet and gore

Clothing and hair carry a mask that drives dirt build-up, stains, mud and wetness from a single material setting, so a character can get muddier through a day of work in the fields or soaked in the rain. The undead versions add a gore layer on top.
