Sir Bedwyr opens the game, so he was the first head through the new pipeline.

## The steps

1. **Seam fit.** The source head is scaled, aligned and cut to meet the body's neck seam with no gap.
2. **Expression topology.** The mesh is rebuilt with edge loops around the eyes and mouth so it can animate.
3. **Mouth.** Teeth, gums, tongue and lips rebuilt and tested at full jaw open.
4. **Skin.** Procedural pores, wrinkles and weathering over a tone taken from the reference, with a broken-nose scar.
5. **Hair.** Long grey hair with a thinning crown, a full grey beard and sideburns, all as card assets.
6. **Face rig.** Head, jaw and eye bones with weights.

## Reference

Bedwyr is 57, gaunt, grey-bearded and one-handed, a companion of Arthur from before Badon. The character bible describes him down to the bend of his badly set nose, and the head was checked against that description at each stage.
