Episode 2

AI Pipeline Improvement for Game Character Development in Unreal Engine

What the first character pipeline got wrong, what changed, and the cast it produced.

An audit of 20 characters on screen counted 1.9 million triangles before shadows, no LODs, and hair cards stacked 7 to 10 layers deep. The rebuild cut character triangles about in half, turned every flaw into one of six automated hair rules, added a ten-step review pass per hero, and replaced dense hair cards with a shell plus strand cards and one shared garment kit.
