Animations

Every move starts as a sentence of plain English and is generated by a text-to-motion model running on a local GPU, then retargeted, cut, measured and fixed by script. Basic movement is in the game for both male bodies; the female set is generated and checked; combat clips are in Unreal and wait for their review pass.
