Animals

Every animal in KnightZ: livestock for the manor, dogs, wild game, birds and fish. 36 animals are rigged and animated in Blender on shared family skeletons, with gaits taken from published measurements where they exist. Each clip passes ground, foot-slide, joint and skin-stretch checks. The ox and cow are in Unreal; the rest go in together in one batch after review.
